Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Case Cracker Club is built around one rule: parents are in control, and kids never have to hand over personal information to play. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, and how it's used.

Who creates the account

Only a parent or legal guardian can create a Case Cracker Club account. At signup, you confirm that you are the parent or legal guardian of any child profile you add, and you choose the email address and password for the account.

What we collect about you (the parent)

  • Your email address and password, used to sign in.
  • A record that you agreed to the parental consent statement at signup, with a timestamp.

What we collect about your child

Every child profile is created by the parent, not the child. A child profile includes only:

  • A codename you choose for them (never their real name).
  • An avatar you or they pick together, either a simple icon or a customizable illustrated character. No photo of your child is ever uploaded or required.
  • Their points and which cases they've solved, so progress and the leaderboard can work.
WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT We never ask for a child's real name, date of birth, photo, school, address, or any other directly identifying information. The leaderboard shows codenames only.

Why we use this information

  • To run the parent dashboard and let each child profile play, track progress, and appear on the codename-only leaderboard.
  • To send account-related messages to the parent's email (like sign-in confirmations).
  • To keep the club fair — for example, making sure each detective gets one real attempt per weekly case.

We do not use this information for advertising, and we do not build profiles of children for marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent any information, ever. A small number of service providers help run the site behind the scenes:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles secure sign-in. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • DiceBear — generates the illustrated avatar characters. Only the avatar style choices you make (like hair or eye color) are sent to render the image; no personal information is included.
  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used on this site.

None of these services receive your child's real name, because we never collect it in the first place.

Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's storage only to keep you signed in between visits. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking scripts.

How long we keep information

We keep account and profile information for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and all associated child profiles deleted, contact us using the details below and we'll take care of it.

Your rights as a parent

  • Review or edit any child profile at any time from your dashboard.
  • Delete a child's profile yourself, or ask us to delete your entire account.
  • Ask us what information we hold about your family, at any time.

A note on children's privacy

Case Cracker Club is designed for use by children, always under a parent-created and parent-managed account. We rely on the parental consent you provide at signup, and we've deliberately limited what any child profile can contain, in keeping with the spirit of laws like COPPA that protect children's privacy online.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let parents know.

Contact us

Questions, requests to review or delete your family's information, or anything else — reach us at hello@casecrackerclub.com.
