FOR PARENTS

Built with kids' privacy first

Case Cracker Club is designed around parent-managed accounts from the ground up.

👪 Parent-managed accounts

You create and control every child profile — kids never enter personal details themselves.

🕵️ No real names on the leaderboard

Kids play under a detective codename, never their real name or photo.

🔒 No data sold, ever

Account info is used only to run the club — never shared with advertisers.

✉️ You're always in the loop

Parents get a summary of what their kid solved each week, by email.
